Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for QR & Barcode Scanner

Last Updated: October 1, 2026 • Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Summary: QR & Barcode Scanner is committed to protecting your privacy. We process camera streams and images locally on your device to scan QR codes and barcodes. We do not store, upload, or transmit your camera feeds, photos, or scan history to any external server. The application displays standard ads served by Google AdMob.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how QR & Barcode Scanner ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you install and use our Android mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. On-Device Camera & Barcode Scanning

The App requests access to your device's camera solely to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes in real time.

  • Strictly Local Processing: Video frames captured by your camera are analyzed locally in volatile memory (RAM) using the open-source ZXing decoding engine.
  • No Cloud Transmission: Camera images and video frames are never recorded, saved to external servers, or transmitted over the internet.
  • Camera access is active only while you are actively viewing the scanner screen.

B. Photo Library & Gallery Scanning

If you choose to scan a barcode from an existing image, the App accesses only the specific photo you choose through the standard Android Photo Picker. The image is parsed locally on your device and is never uploaded.

C. Scan History & Created Codes

Scanned barcodes and custom QR codes you create are stored in a private local SQLite database on your device.

  • All scan records remain solely on your device.
  • You can delete individual history records or clear the entire history at any time from the History screen.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage immediately deletes all stored history data.

3. Android Permissions Requested

The App requests only the permissions necessary for its intended core features:

Permission Purpose
CAMERA Required to capture real-time camera frames for scanning QR codes and barcodes. Processed entirely on-device; never saved or uploaded.
VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback (vibration) when a barcode is detected. Can be disabled in Settings.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to load banner ads and verify network connectivity.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

To deliver relevant advertisements, detect invalid traffic, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain pseudonymous information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Coarse location derived from IP address (e.g., country or city level)
  • Ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, interaction data)

For further information on how Google collects, stores, and uses data, please review Google's official privacy resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Security

Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising

You can control or reset personalized ads directly in your Android device settings:

  1. Open device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not operate remote user databases, account servers, or cloud backups. All scan history belongs to you and is stored locally on your device.

  • You can delete specific items or tap "Clear All" in the History tab to permanently remove all saved items.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all stored application data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. GDPR & California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to specific rights under applicable privacy laws:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can view all saved scan items directly inside the App.
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all your stored data at any time inside the App.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

8. Security

Because all image and barcode processing occurs locally on your device without transmitting video feeds over the internet, your camera data remains strictly private and protected.

9. External Links

If a scanned barcode contains a website link (URL) and you choose to open it, you will navigate to an external website in your browser. We are not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of third-party websites.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
